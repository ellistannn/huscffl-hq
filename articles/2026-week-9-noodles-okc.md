# Can OKC Be the First to Stop New York?

The record is almost too simple: **New York Noodles are 8–0**, and **Good Things We OKC! are 6–2**. Week 9 gives OKC a chance to do something nobody has done this season—beat the defending champions—and gives New York another chance to reinforce its lead.

New York has scored **2,415.83 points**, an average of **301.98 per week**. Its Week 8 victory over NJz Bunniez came with 161.52 points from offense and 130.25 from defense. Josh Allen and Drake Maye supply a strong quarterback tandem; Bhayshul Tuten, Greg Rousseau and Jeffery Simmons proved that the decisive contribution can come from nearly any lineup slot.

OKC’s path is less about matching one big-name quarterback and more about winning the collective defensive battle. **Alex Singleton (28.60), Quentin Lake (25.25) and Roquan Smith (24.75)** all surged in Week 8. The group was the foundation of OKC’s 253.66-point victory over District of Daebak. For the season, OKC averages **256.88**, so it will need to find extra production to keep up with New York’s pace.

The matchup turns on two questions. Can OKC’s IDPs erase the high floor of the Noodles’ offense? And can New York continue to absorb uneven skill-position production without leaving a gap an elite challenger can exploit?

The undefeated record is the prize, but the playoff implications stretch beyond one game. A victory would keep New York clear of the chasing pack; an OKC win would send the title race into a new phase.
