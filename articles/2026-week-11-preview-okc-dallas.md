# OKC Survived by 0.63; Dallas Faces the Week’s Biggest Bye Crisis
*HUSCFFL · Week 11 · Rivalry Week · Published November 20, 2026*

**Good Things We OKC! (7-3) vs. Dalla Dalla Dallas (2-8)**

OKC is 7–3 and remains near the top of the standings, but its last two scores — 199.80 and 186.30 — make the record look sturdier than the recent output. The Week 10 win over Utah came by just 0.63 points. Dallas, at 2–8, was only 8.37 points away from ending New York’s unbeaten season. Both arrive with stories of opportunities narrowly taken or lost.

## The Byepocalypse Test

This is the byepocalypse’s most punishing matchup on paper. Nine Dallas players who started in Week 10 belong to clubs off this week: Deshaun Watson, Kyren Williams, Matthew Golden, KC Concepcion Jr., Dylan Sampson, Za’Darius Smith, Robert Spillane, Nick Emmanwori and Xavier McKinney. That spans both offensive flex depth and defensive positions. In a 20-starter league, simply fielding a legal lineup may require major changes.

OKC is not immune: Week 10 starters AJ Barner, Byron Young, Nate Landman and Quentin Lake also have byes. The Browns, Rams and Seahawks taking the week off matters to both. But OKC’s count is materially lighter, and its 7–3 record means the opportunity to consolidate a playoff position is valuable — even if the final score is ugly.

**Week 10 starter exposure:** Good Things We OKC! had **4** starters from NFL teams on bye; Dalla Dalla Dallas had **9**. These are a retrospective snapshot, not locked Week 11 lineup projections. Managers may already have swapped players or made transactions.

## Injury and Roster Watch

The six-team bye creates immediate holes, while the league-wide quarterback and roster churn makes superflex streaming especially volatile. The previously started Deshaun Watson is on bye regardless of his current health status.

## The Rivalry Stakes

Dallas won the previous meeting 240.93–212.58 in Week 5. That reversal of the standings makes this a meaningful rivalry rematch, not a formality. If Dallas can find replacement scoring and repeat the upset, it will have a signature moment in a difficult season. If OKC fails again to reach 200, the bye crisis might be its escape hatch.

**The question:** Which manager can overcome the combination of rivalry pressure, six NFL teams resting, and a shrinking playoff window?
