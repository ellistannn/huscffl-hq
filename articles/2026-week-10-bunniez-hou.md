# The Bunniez Make a Statement: Houston Runs Into a 298.92-Point Wall
**NJz Bunniez 298.92 · HOU You Like That 236.58 | Final**

If New York's win looked unconvincing, **NJz Bunniez** offered the opposite: a **298.92-point** showcase, the league's highest Week 10 total, and a **62.34-point** dismantling of Houston. The second-place Bunniez moved to **8–2** with a performance powered by the most productive offense of the week. **177.72 offensive points** gave NJz a commanding advantage before the defensive side even entered the picture.

The attacking depth was relentless. **Isaiah Likely** scored **27.70** out of a flex slot, **Jaxon Smith-Njigba** added **27.10**, and the quarterbacks delivered **26.78 from Trevor Lawrence** and **20.44 from C.J. Stroud**. **Devon Witherspoon** matched Likely's **27.70** as the standout defensive back, and **Payton Wilson** contributed **20.25**. NJz did all this despite combining for just **4.25** from three starting defensive linemen: Cameron Heyward, Mason Graham and Nick Bosa. It was an example of tremendous scoring depth rather than a completely flawless lineup.

Houston's quarterbacks performed well enough to win on many weeks. **Dak Prescott (34.42)** and **Joe Burrow (33.16)** combined for **67.58**, while **Amon-Ra St. Brown (23.80)** and **Eric Wilson (22.35)** supplied additional firepower. Still, Houston could not keep pace with NJz's wave of production. **Jalen Coker (0.30)**, **Brandon Cisse (0.75)**, and **Darnell Washington (1.50)** were among the costly quiet spots. Houston's own offense reached **134.08**, a respectable total, but NJz's offense outscored it by **43.64** all by itself.

Houston slips to **3–7**; NJz remains two games behind New York in the standings. One week after both teams looked wildly different in Week 9, this result restored a degree of certainty about the Bunniez: they have the firepower to pressure anyone when their offensive starters erupt together.
