# Rivalry Week Meets the Byepocalypse
*HUSCFFL · 2026 · Week 11*

The schedule could not have timed it more dramatically. With the fantasy season entering its final stretch, **Rivalry Week returns** alongside the six-team NFL bye wave that threatens to scramble lineups across the league. The Falcons, Browns, Packers, Rams, Patriots and Seahawks are off. Those teams account for nearly one in five NFL franchises, leaving significant holes at quarterback, receiver, running back and — in this IDP-heavy league — all across the defense. [Official NFL bye schedule](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-schedule-release-every-team-bye-week).

New York Noodles are undefeated at **10–0**, NJz Bunniez lead the chase at **8–2**, and the middle of the standings faces an increasingly short path to recovery. With twenty starters required per franchise, replacing multiple productive defenders matters every bit as much as shuffling an offense. Lower scores are plausible, but not guaranteed; replacement production and unexpected breakouts could create a week of surprises.

## Five Stories to Watch

### New York Noodles vs. District of Daebak

An undefeated run meets its most complicated lineup week yet. New York enters at 10–0, but the narrow escape against Dallas was a warning: the Noodles survived with only 213.16 points, their lowest total of the season. District, now 3–7, arrives with nothing to lose and every reason to relish a rivalry upset.

New York won the earlier meeting 351.49–243.48 back in Week 2. A similarly comfortable result would be remarkable amid this bye wave. If District wants to turn a 3–7 season into a rivalry spoiler, this may be its most favorable chance to challenge the unbeaten leader.

### Tampa Bay Tyranny vs. Seattle Supersonics

The standings say Seattle is the favorite at 6–4, but Tampa Bay arrives with its best momentum of the year. The Tyranny have scored 241.15, 263.10 and 267.08 over the last three weeks and are coming off a 19.04-point upset of LALALALA. Seattle has won two straight and remains in the upper-middle playoff chase, making this more than a consolation rivalry.

Seattle took their first meeting by only 2.02 points, 263.38–261.36, in Week 4. That is a genuine rivalry grievance waiting to be reopened. Tampa Bay can make the rematch matter by translating a high weekly ranking into a third win; Seattle needs a clean performance to keep pace with the contenders.

### HOU You Like That vs. LALALALA

At 3–7, Houston is running out of runway. Its Week 7 and Week 8 outbursts once made it look dangerous, but a 198.74-point collapse in Week 9 and a 236.58-point loss in Week 10 have left it chasing stability. LALALALA is 5–5 after losing to Tampa Bay, with precious little margin for another setback.

LALALALA won the earlier meeting 275.23–222.79 in Week 5. A repeat would put Houston further behind the playoff pack, while a Houston victory would drag another contender back toward the .500 scrum. Rivalry week gives both teams something concrete to play for.

### Good Things We OKC! vs. Dalla Dalla Dallas

OKC is 7–3 and remains near the top of the standings, but its last two scores — 199.80 and 186.30 — make the record look sturdier than the recent output. The Week 10 win over Utah came by just 0.63 points. Dallas, at 2–8, was only 8.37 points away from ending New York’s unbeaten season. Both arrive with stories of opportunities narrowly taken or lost.

Dallas won the previous meeting 240.93–212.58 in Week 5. That reversal of the standings makes this a meaningful rivalry rematch, not a formality. If Dallas can find replacement scoring and repeat the upset, it will have a signature moment in a difficult season. If OKC fails again to reach 200, the bye crisis might be its escape hatch.

### NJz Bunniez vs. Utah Yetis

NJz Bunniez have been the league’s hottest recent scorer: 217.55 in Week 8, 281.69 in Week 9 and a season-changing 298.92 in Week 10. At 8–2 they are the nearest pursuer of the unbeaten Noodles. Utah, now 4–6, arrives after a brutal 0.63-point loss to OKC and needs every remaining result to matter.

NJz took the first meeting 291.13–227.63 in Week 5. Utah now has a path to a different result: take advantage of the missing NJz stars and survive another week near the playoff bubble. For NJz, winning without its usual headliners would be a convincing statement of championship depth.

## What Decides the Week

It may not be the star with the loudest projection. It may be the manager who identified two playable linebackers, a bye-week receiver, and an emergency second quarterback before the waiver market dried up. The playoff clock is ticking, and the first rivalry week offers no protection for favorites.
