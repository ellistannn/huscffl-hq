# HOU’s Scoring Streak Meets Utah’s Defensive Upside

Both **HOU You Like That** and **Utah Yetis** enter Week 9 at **3–5**, but their trajectories look very different. HOU just topped the entire league with **314.23**. Utah scored **231.16** in a victory over Dallas and will need to find another gear to keep the pace.

There is no mystery about HOU’s recent ceiling. Joe Burrow scored **39.48** against Tampa Bay, Tee Higgins delivered **32.50**, and D’Andre Swift **26.90**. Defensive contributions from T.J. Watt, Eric Wilson and Boye Mafe made the total formidable from top to bottom. This was not a one-off: HOU led Week 7 as well with **296.81**.

Utah has answers of its own, particularly in IDP and at tight end. Alex Anzalone’s **31.75**, Foyesade Oluokun’s **22.50** and Brock Bowers’ **28.20** made the difference against Dallas. The Yetis also had an enormous **39.70** from Amani Hooker and **33.30** from Patrick Queen on the bench, illustrating both the roster’s capability and the difficulty of choosing the right starters.

The strategic question is whether Utah can squeeze more of that defensive upside into its actual lineup. HOU, meanwhile, must prove it can sustain a run of elite weeks without relying on every star hitting simultaneously.

At 3–5, both teams have reason to treat this as a consequential step in the playoff race. The winner gets back toward the center of the standings; the loser risks spending the next few weeks chasing a narrowing opening.
