# Bunniez Chase First Place as Utah Tries to Steal a Lifeline
*HUSCFFL · Week 11 · Rivalry Week · Published November 20, 2026*

**NJz Bunniez (8-2) vs. Utah Yetis (4-6)**

NJz Bunniez have been the league’s hottest recent scorer: 217.55 in Week 8, 281.69 in Week 9 and a season-changing 298.92 in Week 10. At 8–2 they are the nearest pursuer of the unbeaten Noodles. Utah, now 4–6, arrives after a brutal 0.63-point loss to OKC and needs every remaining result to matter.

## The Byepocalypse Test

NJz has substantial exposure to the bye. Its Week 10 lineup featured Bijan Robinson, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Mason Graham, Carson Schwesinger and Devon Witherspoon — five important starters spread across offense and defense. Schwesinger’s 41.15-point Week 9 performance is a reminder that a defensive absence here can matter just as much as a missing wideout.

Utah’s Week 10 starters had only one listed bye player, Byron Murphy II, and thus appear far better positioned to field something close to their previous lineup. That advantage, however, comes with a notable medical caveat: Brock Bowers was listed as questionable for Week 11 with ankle/quad concerns. The status of Utah’s star tight end is especially important in a tight-end-premium format.

**Week 10 starter exposure:** NJz Bunniez had **5** starters from NFL teams on bye; Utah Yetis had **1**. These are a retrospective snapshot, not locked Week 11 lineup projections. Managers may already have swapped players or made transactions.

## Injury and Roster Watch

Brock Bowers is listed as questionable entering Sunday’s Raiders–Broncos game; Utah should monitor his activation and be prepared with a late-window alternative. [Bowers status](https://www.nbcsports.com/fantasy/football/player-news/2026-11-20/brock-bowers-ankle-quad-questionable-in-week-11).

## The Rivalry Stakes

NJz took the first meeting 291.13–227.63 in Week 5. Utah now has a path to a different result: take advantage of the missing NJz stars and survive another week near the playoff bubble. For NJz, winning without its usual headliners would be a convincing statement of championship depth.

**The question:** Which manager can overcome the combination of rivalry pressure, six NFL teams resting, and a shrinking playoff window?
