# Ten and Counting, but Byes Threaten the Perfect Season
*HUSCFFL · Week 11 · Rivalry Week · Published November 20, 2026*

**New York Noodles (10-0) vs. District of Daebak (3-7)**

An undefeated run meets its most complicated lineup week yet. New York enters at 10–0, but the narrow escape against Dallas was a warning: the Noodles survived with only 213.16 points, their lowest total of the season. District, now 3–7, arrives with nothing to lose and every reason to relish a rivalry upset.

## The Byepocalypse Test

The challenge for New York is not merely replacing one absent star. Its Week 10 starters included Drake London, Denzel Boston, Ernest Jones IV, Xavier Watts and Julian Love, all on Week 11 byes, plus Drake Maye, whose ankle injury has become a larger concern than a single off-week. This strains receiver, superflex and the defensive backfield simultaneously. Josh Allen and the remaining healthy starters may need to compensate, but a deep dynasty bench faces a real stress test.

District has an opportunity of its own, although Matthew Stafford, Blake Corum, Hunter Henry and Jordan Love were among its Week 10 starters who will not play this week. Losing both previously used quarterbacks is particularly awkward in a superflex format. With Jordan Love fresh off a 36.18-point Week 9, replacing that scoring ceiling is no routine task. Both managers face an unusual question: who can put together the least compromised lineup?

**Week 10 starter exposure:** New York Noodles had **6** starters from NFL teams on bye; District of Daebak had **4**. These are a retrospective snapshot, not locked Week 11 lineup projections. Managers may already have swapped players or made transactions.

## Injury and Roster Watch

NBC Sports reported that Drake Maye suffered an ankle injury with fears of a season-ending absence; the Patriots also have a Week 11 bye. The long-term superflex implications deserve attention. ## The Rivalry Stakes

New York won the earlier meeting 351.49–243.48 back in Week 2. A similarly comfortable result would be remarkable amid this bye wave. If District wants to turn a 3–7 season into a rivalry spoiler, this may be its most favorable chance to challenge the unbeaten leader.

**The question:** Which manager can overcome the combination of rivalry pressure, six NFL teams resting, and a shrinking playoff window?
