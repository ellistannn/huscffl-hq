High Up Social Club Fantasy Football League (HUSCFFL) Constitution

Version: 1.1

Effective Date: July 4th, 2025
Updated: July 31st, 2026

I. League Overview

1.1 The High Up Social Club Fantasy Football League (HUSCFFL) is a 10-team dynasty fantasy football league focused on long-term team building, strategic gameplay, and community engagement.

1.2 This constitution outlines the structure, rules, and expectations for all aspects of the league.

1.3 The league is hosted on Sleeper.

1.4 The league commissioner, hereafter referred to as “The Commish”, is Ellis Tan.

II. League Structure

2.1 Format & Settings:

· League Type: Dynasty

· Scoring System: Standard ½-PPR (0.5 per reception); Tight End Premium (1.0 PPR for TEs); IDP Big 3 Scoring

· Roster Type: Superflex TEP + IDP

· Number of Teams: 10

2.2 Starting Roster:

· Offense:

o 1 QB

o 2 RB

o 3 WR

o 1 TE

o 2 FLEX (RB/WR/TE)

o 1 SUPERFLEX (QB/RB/WR/TE)

· Defense (IDP):

o 3 DL

o 3 LB

o 3 DB

o 1 IDP FLEX (DL/LB/DB)

2.3 Reserve Roster:

· Bench: 25 players

· Injured Reserve (IR): 4 spots (IR/PUP eligible)

· Taxi Squad:

o 5 rookies only

o Must be assigned before Week 1

o Must be removed after 2 years of NFL experience

2.4 Schedule:

· Regular Season: Weeks 1–14

· Playoffs: Weeks 15–17

o 6 Teams qualify

o Top 2 seeds get a Week 15 bye

· Toilet Bowl: Seeds 7–10 compete

o Loser in Week 17 = Last Place

2.5 Team Names:

· All team names must include a real geographic reference (city, state, neighborhood, region) that reflects the manager’s identity

Example Team Names:

· Brooklyn Bruisers (Neighborhood)

· Seoul Smashers (City)

· Bay Area Bunnies (Region)

· Orange County Orangutans (County)

· Nevada Nellys (State)

· The Boogie Down Bruisers (The Bronx)

· DMV Dynasty (DC/Maryland/Virginia)

· Chitown Crushers (Chicago)

· The Big Easy Brawlers (New Orleans)

· SoCal Shockers (Southern California)

2.6 Team Name Changes:

· Managers may request to change their team name once per season & offseason

· All team names must still comply with Section 2.5 (must include a real geographic reference). · Team name change requests must be submitted to The Commish.

· The updated name will be reflected in the Team Directory section.

· Previous names will be retained in parentheses for historical tracking.

IV. Draft & Pick Management

4.1 Annual Draft Format:

· Dynasty-style Supplemental Draft

· 5 rounds

· Includes NFL rookies and un-rostered free agents

· Hosted on Sleeper

· 12-hour time limit per pick (slow draft)

4.2 Draft Order Determination:

· Picks 1–4: Determined via Max PF of the bottom 4 teams

o Lowest Max PF receives Pick 1, 2nd lowest receives Pick 2

o Highest Max PF receives Pick 4, 2nd highest receives Pick 3

· Picks 5–10: Determined by playoff finish

o Champion = Pick 10, Runner-up = Pick 9, etc.

4.3 Pick Trading:

· Draft picks (current & future) are fully tradable

· Managers trading away future draft picks (for a year beyond the current season) must pre-pay a minimum of $10 for each year beyond the current season at the time of the trade. The pre-payment will be credited towards that respective season’s league dues, and the difference will be returned to the manager in the event of the league dues being less than the pre-paid amount.

· This ensures long-term league integrity and commitment

V. Transactions

5.1 Trades:

· Deadline: Week 14

· Review Period: Max 24 hours

· Trades processed unless flagged for collusion or league-breaking

· The Commish may veto such trades with explanation

5.2 Waivers:

· FAAB System: $100 budget per team each season ($0 minimum)

· Processing Time

o After Game Played – Wednesday at 3:00 AM ET

o After Player Dropped – 48 hours

o Daily on Thursday/Friday/Saturday at 3:00 AM ET

· Free agent period opens on Sundays and Mondays (first-come, first-served)

VI. The Stakes

6.1 League Dues:

· Collected before the start of each season

· Amount set by The Commish based on league operating costs (e.g. tools, trophies, platform)

6.2 Prize Pool System:

· Multiplier system based on final standings

· The last place finisher pays the multiplier x 16.

· Multiplier set by majority vote before each season

· Payments due upon season completion

· First place receives 75%

· Second place receives 20%

· Third place pays no dues

6.3 Last Place Punishment

· The winner of the Toilet Bowl must face a punishment determined prior to the season start.

o 2026 Punishment - Donation matching amount owed to charity of choice

6.3 Trophies & Awards:

· Perpetual Trophy:

o Engraved with winner’s name, team name, and year

o Sent to winner until next champion is crowned

· Trading Card Slabs:

o Custom card created for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place

o Designed in cooperation with winning managers & distributed by The Commish

· Discord Roles:

o 🏆 HUSCFFL Champion (1st Place)

o 💩 king (Toilet Bowl Loser)

o Held until the next season’s results

VII. League Dynasties & Reset Clause

7.1 Trigger Conditions: A full league reset and redraft will occur if a team:

· Wins 3 consecutive championships, OR

· Wins 3 championships within 5 seasons

7.2 Reset Procedures:

· Full redraft of all players

· All rosters, benches, IRs, and taxi squads cleared

· Draft picks voided

· New draft order created by The Commish + league vote

7.3 Override Option:

· Reset may be vetoed only by unanimous league vote

VIII. Governance & Rule Changes

8.1 Rule Proposals:

· Can be submitted by any manager during the offseason

· Require majority approval (6/10) to pass

8.2 Midseason Rule Changes:

· Require unanimous league approval

8.3 Final Authority:

· The Commish has authority on all matters unless overridden by majority vote (or unanimous for midseason changes)

· All decisions should prioritize fairness, transparency, and fun

IX. Conduct & Activity Expectations

9.1 Participation:

· Managers must set a legal lineup each week

· Inactive managers or tanking in an egregious manner will be warned and a penalty may be incurred

9.2 Behavior:

· Respectful conduct is expected in all league chats and interactions

· Toxicity, harassment, or abuse may result in penalties or removal

X. Manager Withdrawal & Replacement

10.1 Voluntary Exit

· A manager may leave the league at any time with notice to The Commish.

· It is strongly preferred they finish out the current season before stepping away.

· They must make a good faith effort to help facilitate the transition to a new manager.

10.2 Finding a Replacement

· The departing manager may suggest a replacement.

· The Commish will review and approve replacements based on league fit and commitment.

· If no replacement is found by the start of the next season, The Commish will recruit a new manager from trusted sources.

10.3 Roster Transition Options

· Incoming managers must take over the existing roster as is.

· Optionally, The Commish may offer minor incentives to new managers (e.g., future 3rd rounder) if the roster is severely depleted

10.4 Emergency Takeover

· If a manager becomes unresponsive, The Commish may:

o Set lineups to maintain competitive fairness

o Initiate a replacement search immediately

o Lock the team from making further moves
