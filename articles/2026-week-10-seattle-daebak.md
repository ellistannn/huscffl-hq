# Seattle Stays Above Water as District's Slide Continues
**Seattle Supersonics 249.30 · District of Daebak 230.51 | Final**

Seattle did not match the explosiveness of its Week 9 rebound, but it didn't need to. District's defense totaled only **98.75**, trailing Seattle by **18.65** — virtually the entire winning margin.

Seattle's back-to-back wins have given it some separation in a crowded chase. District, meanwhile, is discovering how little individual brilliance matters without enough depth across all twenty starters.
