# Tyranny Finds Momentum as Seattle Faces a Shorthanded Test
*HUSCFFL · Week 11 · Rivalry Week · Published November 20, 2026*

**Tampa Bay Tyranny (2-8) vs. Seattle Supersonics (6-4)**

The standings say Seattle is the favorite at 6–4, but Tampa Bay arrives with its best momentum of the year. The Tyranny have scored 241.15, 263.10 and 267.08 over the last three weeks and are coming off a 19.04-point upset of LALALALA. Seattle has won two straight and remains in the upper-middle playoff chase, making this more than a consolation rivalry.

## The Byepocalypse Test

The matchup turns on defensive replacements and wide-receiver depth. Tampa Bay had Myles Garrett, Lukas Van Ness and Evan Williams among its Week 10 starters, all now on bye, along with Tucker Kraft at tight end and Corey Kiner at running back. Losing Garrett after last week’s lineup regrets is particularly irritating: the very player a manager wished had started in Week 9 is unavailable now.

Seattle is similarly squeezed. Quinshon Judkins, Christian Watson, Konata Mumpfield, Aaron Donald and Keisean Nixon occupied starting slots in Week 10 but will not play this week. Puka Nacua was on Seattle’s Week 10 bench; his knee surgery adds a long-term roster concern beyond the bye. For a team trying to remain above .500, assembling enough receiving and IDP production is an important test.

## The Rivalry Stakes

Seattle took their first meeting by only 2.02 points, 263.38–261.36, in Week 4. That is a genuine rivalry grievance waiting to be reopened. Tampa Bay can make the rematch matter by translating a high weekly ranking into a third win; Seattle needs a clean performance to keep pace with the contenders.

**The question:** Which manager can overcome the combination of rivalry pressure, six NFL teams resting, and a shrinking playoff window?
