# Week 8 Roundup: The Unbeaten Leader and a New Scoring Surge

Week 8 was a study in separation. **New York Noodles reached 8–0**, two challengers stayed at **6–2**, and the league’s most explosive team of the week was sitting below .500. As the playoff race takes shape, scoring strength and win-loss records are telling different stories.

The headline matchup became the week’s largest blowout. New York defeated **NJz Bunniez 291.77–217.55**, a 74.22-point margin that reinforced its position atop the table. **Good Things We OKC!** kept pace at 6–2 by beating **District of Daebak 253.66–200.55**, with Alex Singleton, Quentin Lake and Roquan Smith supplying standout defensive production.

Then came **HOU You Like That’s 314.23**, the highest total in the league this week, against **Tampa Bay Tyranny’s 241.15**. Joe Burrow’s 39.48 and Tee Higgins’ 32.50 led a lineup that also received substantial production from T.J. Watt and Eric Wilson. HOU is only 3–5, but it has outscored every other franchise in each of the last two weeks.

Elsewhere, **Utah Yetis defeated Dalla Dalla Dallas 231.16–195.06**, as Alex Anzalone and Brock Bowers helped keep Utah within reach of the playoff chase. **LALALALA beat Seattle Supersonics 234.73–202.37** to pull even with Seattle at 4–4. That was the closest matchup of the week, despite the 32.36-point margin.

The standings now feature the **Noodles (8–0)**, **Bunniez (6–2)** and **OKC (6–2)** in the top three. LALALALA and Seattle stand at **4–4**; HOU, District and Utah are **3–5**; Dallas is **2–6**; and Tampa Bay is **1–7**. There is still plenty of season left, but the margins for recovery are shrinking.

Week 9 brings an intriguing test at both ends of the race. New York faces OKC in a clash near the top, while **District of Daebak meets LALALALA** with playoff positioning—and a few manager challenges—on the line.
