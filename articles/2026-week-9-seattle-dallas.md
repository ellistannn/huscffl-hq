# Seattle and Dallas Search for a Turning Point

There may not be a trophy on the line, but **Seattle Supersonics (4–4)** and **Dalla Dalla Dallas (2–6)** both need a change in direction. Seattle has fallen from earlier scoring highs, while Dallas is running out of comfortable paths back into the playoff conversation.

Seattle’s Week 8 loss to LALALALA produced **202.37** points. Kirk Cousins was the standout with **28.18**, Jahmyr Gibbs added **23.80**, and Dane Belton registered **22.00**. But the broader starting group lacked the depth to compete. Seattle has now scored **206.53, 214.57 and 202.37** in its last three games, a notable decline from its **309.30** in Week 3.

Dallas also struggled in Week 8, losing **231.16–195.06** to Utah. Andrew Van Ginkel provided **27.40**, Bo Nix **19.28** and Matthew Golden **17.40**, but too many positions delivered ordinary totals. Trent McDuffie’s **26.10** on the bench offers an example of production the club would love to see in its starting lineup.

The question for Seattle is whether a more complete performance from its offensive depth can restore the team’s earlier threat level. For Dallas, the challenge is getting enough meaningful contributions on offense and defense at the same time.

Seattle can move above .500 with a win. Dallas can begin to reverse a difficult first half. Neither side can afford to assume the other will solve its problems for it.
