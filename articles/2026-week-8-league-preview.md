# Week 8 Preview: First Place Goes on the Line

Seven weeks created a hierarchy. Week 8 puts it under pressure. **New York Noodles (7–0) vs. NJz Bunniez (6–1) is the HUSCFFL Matchup of the Week**, a direct meeting of the only two teams with six or more wins. The Noodles have been the league’s standard, but the Bunniez have shown the explosive skill-position play needed to threaten them.

The stakes extend beyond the headliner. Good Things We OKC! (5–2) can make ground on one of the leaders. Seattle wants to restore its momentum after running into HOU’s record-setting Week 7 outburst. At the bottom of the table, multiple managers are facing opponents who can either help rescue their seasons or push them further out of the race.

## The Full Week 8 Slate

- **New York Noodles (7–0) vs. NJz Bunniez (6–1)** — The showdown that has been building since the opening weeks is finally here. The unbeaten Noodles have scored 2,124.06 through seven games, while the Bunniez have turned a 6–1 start into the only immediate threat to New York’s control of first place.
- **Tampa Bay Tyranny (1–6) vs. HOU You Like That (2–5)** — One team arrives in search of its second victory; the other arrives with the confidence of the highest single-week score in the league. Tampa is 1–6, HOU 2–5, yet the difference in recent momentum feels larger than one win.
- **Good Things We OKC! (5–2) vs. District of Daebak (3–4)** — OKC has settled into third at 5–2, while Daebak is trying to convert an encouraging 3–4 record into a climb toward the playoff picture. This is a test of whether OKC can keep pace with the top two and whether Daebak can consistently beat strong opposition.
- **LALALALA (3–4) vs. Seattle Supersonics (4–3)** — Seattle returns at 4–3 after taking an 82.24-point hit from HOU. LALALALA is 3–4 despite scoring more cumulative points than some teams above it. This matchup offers each manager a route toward turning a mixed season into a stronger second half.
- **Utah Yetis (2–5) vs. Dalla Dalla Dallas (2–5)** — Two 2–5 clubs face one another in a game where the standings will not fully capture the urgency. Utah is trying to escape a week in which it scored only 175.28. Dallas is trying to forget that one linebacker decision could have turned an 11.82-point loss into a win.

## Matchup of the Week: Noodles vs. Bunniez

The headline deserves its own feature. **New York has scored 2,124.06 through seven games (303.44 points per game)**; the Bunniez have scored **1,901.90 (271.70 per game)**. The Noodles won by 102.39 in Week 7, while NJz survived by 11.82. That contrast is meaningful, but cannot tell the whole story of a fresh matchup.

The Bunniez have the skill-position ceiling to test New York: Tony Pollard (35.00) and Jaxon Smith-Njigba (26.80) were the leading Week 7 examples. The Noodles countered with Drake Maye (27.44), Trey McBride (27.10) and Aidan Hutchinson (24.25), with 124.75 total points from their starting IDP unit. The question is not simply who has the bigger star: which manager can avoid the low-scoring starters and make the right defensive choices?

Most tellingly, the Bunniez left Blake Cashman’s 53.40 on the bench last week, while New York left Yaya Diaby’s 33.60 unused. Both survived those decisions. Against each other, there may be less forgiveness.

## Other Storylines Worth Your Attention

**Can HOU make its breakthrough last?** Its 296.81 against Seattle was the week’s highest score. A win over 1–6 Tampa would turn a spectacular one-off into tangible momentum. Conversely, Tampa has an opportunity to make a statement against a team suddenly riding high.

**OKC and Daebak could be decided by linebackers.** OKC’s Alex Singleton scored 34.65 in Week 7; Daebak had Quinnen Williams and DeMarvion Overshown above 20. This format makes a defensive eruption potentially as decisive as a multi-touchdown offensive day.

**Seattle and LALALALA are fighting over trajectory.** At 4–3 and 3–4, respectively, both have demonstrated productive lineup combinations. The winner will have more room to absorb later adversity.

**Dallas and Utah are chasing recovery.** Both stand at 2–5. Dallas needs its defensive starts to support an efficient offense; Utah needs more complete output across 20 positions after several Week 7 zeroes.

## What to Watch as the Week Concludes

The first question will be the winner of Noodles–Bunniez; the second will be whether the underlying performances affirm each team’s reputation. Monitor offensive and defensive splits, bench points left unused, and the actual starters. Those details can be more instructive than a standalone final score.
