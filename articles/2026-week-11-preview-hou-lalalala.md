# Houston Looks for a Late-Season Spark Against a Fragile .500 Rival
*HUSCFFL · Week 11 · Rivalry Week · Published November 20, 2026*

**HOU You Like That (3-7) vs. LALALALA (5-5)**

At 3–7, Houston is running out of runway. Its Week 7 and Week 8 outbursts once made it look dangerous, but a 198.74-point collapse in Week 9 and a 236.58-point loss in Week 10 have left it chasing stability. LALALALA is 5–5 after losing to Tampa Bay, with precious little margin for another setback.

## The Byepocalypse Test

The bye math appears less brutal than for other teams, though it is not painless. Houston’s Week 10 starters included Seattle running back Zach Charbonnet, Packers defensive back Brandon Cisse and Seattle linebacker Drake Thomas, all unavailable. A shortage at linebacker may hurt more in this IDP-heavy league than the casual fantasy manager expects.

LALALALA had fewer prior starters on bye: Cleveland tight end Harold Fannin Jr. and linebacker Quincy Williams. That relative roster flexibility could be a decisive advantage. But availability is not the same thing as production; last week’s 248.04 was good enough to beat several teams, yet not Tampa Bay. The offense must be consistent while the defense works around its missing starters.

**Week 10 starter exposure:** HOU You Like That had **3** starters from NFL teams on bye; LALALALA had **2**. These are a retrospective snapshot, not locked Week 11 lineup projections. Managers may already have swapped players or made transactions.

## Injury and Roster Watch

Compared with the six-bye burden elsewhere, this matchup may reward active waiver decisions more than drastic personnel overhauls. Check final injury designations before setting the IDP-flex and superflex spots.

## The Rivalry Stakes

LALALALA won the earlier meeting 275.23–222.79 in Week 5. A repeat would put Houston further behind the playoff pack, while a Houston victory would drag another contender back toward the .500 scrum. Rivalry week gives both teams something concrete to play for.

**The question:** Which manager can overcome the combination of rivalry pressure, six NFL teams resting, and a shrinking playoff window?
