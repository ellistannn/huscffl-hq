# HOU Hits the Gas: Week’s Highest Score Buries Tyranny

Nobody scored more in Week 8 than **HOU You Like That**, whose **314.23–241.15** victory over Tampa Bay Tyranny was a reminder that its **3–5** record does not describe the full roster’s ceiling. After posting **296.81** in Week 7, HOU has now produced back-to-back league-leading weekly totals.

Joe Burrow was the centerpiece with **39.48**, while teammate Tee Higgins scored **32.50** and D’Andre Swift contributed **26.90**. The defensive starters amplified the surge: T.J. Watt recorded **28.20**, Eric Wilson **26.50**, and Boye Mafe **21.90**. HOU was not riding one position group; it was receiving winning production at nearly every level.

Tampa Bay had legitimate bright spots. Ja’Marr Chase scored **27.10**, Patrick Mahomes produced **25.44** in the superflex, and Lukas Van Ness added **20.55**. Yet those contributions could not match HOU’s deep collection of standout scores. The Tyranny fall to **1–7**, an especially harsh record for a roster that has shown competitive moments this year.

There was potential for even more from HOU: Cam Ward recorded **26.26** on the bench, although the starters already comfortably won. For Tampa Bay, Chop Robinson’s unused **23.20** stands out. Neither decision changes the dominant shape of the matchup.

HOU’s next obstacle is Utah Yetis. The real question is whether the last two weeks signal a sustainable second-half transformation or simply a tremendous scoring run. If the offensive stars and defensive front keep answering together, HOU can remain a dangerous opponent regardless of the standings.
