# The Perfect Season Meets Desperation
*HUSCFFL · 2026 · Week 10*

Nine weeks are complete and one franchise has yet to lose. **New York Noodles (9–0)** are still undefeated, but Week 10 offers four additional reasons for managers to pay attention: a bruised contender, a volatile challenger, a rebuilding last-place club, and two teams trying to make the jump from the playoff pack into genuine contention.

## The Week 10 Slate

- **New York Noodles (9–0) vs. Dalla Dalla Dallas (2–7)** — Unbeaten New York faces a struggling Dallas club with the season’s widest difference in standings position.
- **Tampa Bay Tyranny (1–8) vs. LALALALA (5–4)** — LALALALA hopes to build on its featured Week 9 victory while Tampa Bay hunts an elusive second win.
- **HOU You Like That (3–6) vs. NJz Bunniez (7–2)** — Houston seeks a rebound after a 115.49-point scoring slide; NJz needs a win to keep the top seed within reach.
- **Good Things We OKC! (6–3) vs. Utah Yetis (4–5)** — The third-place club faces a Utah side that has won its way back within one game of .500.
- **Seattle Supersonics (5–4) vs. District of Daebak (3–6)** — Seattle is looking for consecutive victories; Daebak must turn promising individual performances into team-level results.

## Editor's Spotlight: HOU You Like That vs. NJz Bunniez

This is an editorial selection, not a league-designated Matchup of the Week. **NJz (7–2)** has a firm hold on second place while **Houston (3–6)** is the wild card with the largest recent scoring range. Houston posted **296.81 in Week 7**, an even better **314.23 in Week 8**, then crashed to **198.74 in Week 9**. The Bunniez just scored **281.69**, with the league's top Week 9 offense (**143.04**) plus **138.65 on defense**. If Houston can rediscover its Week 7–8 ceiling, this could be the week's biggest surprise. If it cannot, NJz has an opportunity to stay within reach of New York.

## Five Stories That Could Shape the Standings

### How long can New York keep this going?

The Noodles' **302.01 points per game** leads the league. Dallas enters at **2–7** and most recently managed **202.64**, but the task remains the same for New York: prevent an opponent from stealing a win through an outsized IDP afternoon or an uncharacteristically flat offensive performance. The earlier 2026 meeting ended **308.59–233.73** in New York's favor.

### Can Tampa Bay turn competitive scoring into a win?

Tampa Bay ranked **fifth in Week 9 total points (263.10)** and still lost. Its **147.70 defensive points** ranked third among all teams. LALALALA counters with **169.35 defensive points**, second overall, and enters on the back of its victory in the featured Week 9 contest. The defensive matchups are compelling even with the disparity in records.

### Which OKC shows up against Utah?

OKC is **6–3** but stumbled to **199.80 points** against New York. Utah, now **4–5**, scored **248.34** last week and had the second-highest offense at **139.64**. The season-long scoring numbers favor OKC, but Utah brings the better immediate form.

### Can Seattle make its rebound real?

Seattle improved from **202.37 in Week 8** to **274.74 in Week 9**, while District of Daebak lost its featured matchup despite a **36.18-point** showing from Jordan Love. Seattle is **5–4**; District is **3–6**. Another complete Seattle performance could add separation; an upset would pull District closer to relevance.

### Who benefits from the next close finish?

Tampa Bay's **18.59-point** loss to NJz last week was the closest contest. A single productive IDP substitution could have changed the result, illustrating how costly small lineup choices are in HUSCFFL. The same principle will apply to all five games in Week 10.

## What to Watch

The headline is New York's pursuit of **10–0**, but the bigger playoff picture may turn on NJz's ability to avoid Houston's upside, whether Utah can reach .500, and whether Seattle and LALALALA separate themselves from the teams still scrambling for position.
