# Noodles Turn a Rivalry Into a Rout

**Final: New York Noodles 277.67, Utah Yetis 175.28. Margin: 102.39 points.** What began as a normal Week 7 matchup ended as the largest blowout on the slate. The Noodles didn't reach the week's top overall score, but they produced more than enough to remain undefeated at **7–0**, while Utah fell to **2–5**.

## New York Noodles: Contributions Everywhere

It wasn't a one-man performance. **Drake Maye (27.44)** provided quarterback stability, **Trey McBride (27.10)** delivered at tight end, and **Aidan Hutchinson (24.25)** gave the defense an anchor. Geno Smith (23.48) and Tykee Smith (21.00) added more strong outings. The Noodles received **152.92 offensive points and 124.75 defensive points**, illustrating how difficult this roster can be to contain under the league's IDP-heavy scoring format.

There is one surprising footnote to the convincing win: Yaya Diaby (33.60), Jessie Bates III (26.25) and Jamien Sherwood (23.00) all scored heavily on the bench. The published optimal-lineup analysis estimated **89.45 points left on the bench**. It didn't matter against Utah, but that gap is worth monitoring in closer contests.

## Utah Yetis: A Difficult Week With Some Bright Spots

Utah's **175.28** was the lowest team total of the five matchups. Still, **Patrick Queen's 26.55**, **Bucky Irving's 23.50** and **Rhamondre Stevenson's 20.00** gave the Yetis legitimate contributions. The trouble was the overall depth of the scoring response against a Noodles team that piled up quality starts across positions.

Utah moved to **2–5**, with **1,446.63 points for** through seven weeks. The goal now is to find a more consistent full-lineup output rather than relying on a handful of strong individual scores.

## What This Means

New York's unbeaten streak remains the headline, but this victory was as much about roster balance as star power. Utah has to turn isolated successes into an entire team's worth of production. Both managers will come away with very different reasons to study the lineup decisions from Week 7.
