# Bunniez Seek a Reset Against Reeling Tyranny

**NJz Bunniez (6–2)** enter Week 9 at an unfamiliar crossroads after a 74.22-point defeat to New York Noodles. Their next opponent, **Tampa Bay Tyranny (1–7)**, has a much more urgent problem: the wins are not following the roster’s isolated bright spots.

The Bunniez still have credible offensive pillars. Tony Pollard scored **27.20** in Week 8, Jaxon Smith-Njigba added **20.80**, and the duo of Kyler Murray and Trevor Lawrence combined for **39.30**. Those numbers did not get NJz close to New York, but they offer a stable platform against a different opponent.

Tampa Bay was overpowered by HOU despite Ja’Marr Chase scoring **27.10**, Patrick Mahomes **25.44** and Lukas Van Ness **20.55**. The Tyranny managed **241.15**, a total that would have been competitive in other Week 8 matchups. That contrast is a useful caution for NJz: Tampa Bay’s 1–7 record is not a reason to coast.

The hidden battle is on defense. Both teams left productive options on the bench last week—Cameron Heyward for NJz and Chop Robinson for Tampa Bay—while the Tyranny’s defensive group needs more scores like Van Ness’s to create pressure.

For NJz, a win protects its position among the top contenders and begins to put the New York defeat behind it. For Tampa Bay, this is a chance to make the last part of the season about more than the standings.
