# Thanksgiving Came Early: Rivalry Week 11 Took Its Toll
*HUSCFFL · 2026 · Week 11 league roundup*

The first rivalry week of the closing stretch turned into an exercise in identity. The NFL's six-team byepocalypse reduced available depth across the league, but the real divide was not simply who lost the most starters—it was which franchise could improvise the best 20-player lineup.

{{week11_recap_scorecards}}

## The Week in Five Headlines

**Utah shattered the favorite's confidence.** The Yetis' 248.04–184.30 takedown of NJz ended the week's biggest mismatch with the underdog on top. NJz remains 8–3, but OKC now owns an identical record.

**New York is 11–0 and the defense is the headline.** The Noodles turned just 99.00 offensive points into a 295.20 final because the IDP unit exploded for 196.20. George Karlaftis (37.75), Jeffery Simmons (34.35) and David Bailey (32.65) alone supplied 104.75.

**Houston played like a contender again.** Its 275.10 against LALALALA ranked second league-wide, and 153.10 from the offense set the week's pace in that half of the lineup.

**Seattle learned to win without a hot offense.** A defense-led 235.76–214.84 victory over Tampa Bay was good enough to strengthen fourth place at 7–4.

**OKC regained control.** After struggling under 200 in both Weeks 9 and 10, its 247.44 against Dallas provided important breathing room atop the crowded chasing pack.

New York's 196.20 towered over Seattle's 138.80 and District's 128.20. In a league with ten defensive starting slots, IDP is not an accessory; it is often the whole story.

## The Matchup Reports

### New York Noodles 295.20, District of Daebak 243.38

The undefeated run survived by 51.82. New York's defensive avalanche overcame its sub-100 offense, while District was left to reconcile a respectable 243.38 with another loss. The rival's 3–8 record now looks more desperate than its underlying scoring quality.

### Good Things We OKC! 247.44, Dalla Dalla Dallas 198.11

A 49.33-point victory steadied OKC's pursuit of the first-round bye. Dallas could not keep pace, and the timing could not have been worse as the consolation bracket looms.

### HOU You Like That 275.10, LALALALA 229.33

Dak Prescott (35.90) and Boye Mafe (34.85) were the signature performers for Houston. A decisive 45.77-point gap sends LALALALA below .500 and reinforces the sense that Houston's 4–7 record undersells its weekly ceiling.

### Utah Yetis 248.04, NJz Bunniez 184.30

The headline upset by 63.74 was also the week's largest blowout. Utah showed that the playoff race can still change dramatically with one well-timed rivalry result.

### Seattle Supersonics 235.76, Tampa Bay Tyranny 214.84

Seattle found just 96.96 offense but 138.80 defense. Tampa Bay's 145.44 offense could not mask its defensive shortfall, underscoring the asymmetry of its roster.

## The Week 11 Thanksgiving Awards

- **The Thanksgiving Table Setter — New York Noodles:** 295.20 points and an 11–0 mark—no one takes the head of this table.
- **The Giant Slayer — Utah Yetis:** A 63.74-point demolition of NJz, the week’s biggest margin.
- **The Best Counterpunch — HOU You Like That:** 275.10 points after a badly needed turnaround.
- **The Defensive Feast — New York Noodles:** 196.20 defensive points, more than NJz scored as an entire team.
- **The Empty Plate — NJz Bunniez:** 184.30 points, the league’s weekly low after entering 8–2.
- **The Narrowest Escape — Seattle Supersonics:** A 20.92-point win was the closest completed rivalry game of the week.
- **The Wrong Side of the Wishbone — Tampa Bay Tyranny:** 145.44 offensive points went unrewarded by a defense that managed 69.40.
- **The Playoff Squeeze — LALALALA:** A loss drops the franchise to 5–6 while Utah climbs to the same record.
- **The Resurgence — Good Things We OKC!:** 247.44 points after consecutive scores of 199.80 and 186.30.
- **The IDP Parade — George Karlaftis, Jeffery Simmons and David Bailey:** Three Noodles defenders combined for 104.75 points.

## Standings at the Holiday Turn

**New York Noodles (11–0)** still leads; **NJz and OKC (8–3)** are tied in record; **Seattle (7–4)** is fourth. **LALALALA and Utah (5–6)** hold the fifth and sixth places, with Houston (4–7) chasing and District (3–8), Tampa Bay (2–9) and Dallas (2–9) increasingly pressed for time. The six playoff spots are still the line every franchise is watching, but formal clinching should be governed by the live site's mathematics, not presumed from this recap.

Rivalry Week returns immediately on Thanksgiving, when the first NFL kickoff lands Wednesday evening rather than Thursday. That head start will make preparation—and setting lineups before the holiday meal—part of the competition.
