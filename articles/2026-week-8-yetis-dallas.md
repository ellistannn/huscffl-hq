# Yetis Find a Lifeline Against Dallas

**Utah Yetis earned a much-needed 231.16–195.06 victory over Dalla Dalla Dallas**, a 36.10-point result that lifts Utah to **3–5** and drops Dallas to **2–6**. In a season where neither club has had much breathing room, Utah found a dependable formula: tight end production and a defense that could turn tackles into meaningful fantasy points.

Alex Anzalone led Utah’s starters with **31.75**, Brock Bowers delivered **28.20** at tight end, and Foyesade Oluokun contributed **22.50** in the IDP flex. Bucky Irving also reached **21.00**, providing the kind of backfield support that has not always been available to the Yetis.

Dallas got a strong **27.40** from Andrew Van Ginkel, plus **19.28** from Bo Nix and **17.40** from Matthew Golden. But the offense did not find enough additional impact performers to challenge Utah’s lead. The result leaves Dallas with increasingly urgent work to do as the second half advances.

Both managers will have reasons to revisit the bench. Utah’s Amani Hooker scored **39.70** and Patrick Queen **33.30** outside the starting lineup. Dallas left Trent McDuffie’s **26.10** unused. Utah’s calculated optimal-to-actual gap was **98.60 points**, so even in victory the team has substantial room to improve its lineup deployment.

The immediate challenge for the Yetis is much tougher: they face a HOU You Like That squad coming off the week’s highest score. Dallas meets Seattle Supersonics in a game that could define whether its late-season recovery remains plausible.
