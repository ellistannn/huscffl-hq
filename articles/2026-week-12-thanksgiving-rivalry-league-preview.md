# Five Rivalries, Five Days, One Thanksgiving Table
*HUSCFFL · 2026 · Week 12 league preview · Thanksgiving special*

It is the second consecutive rivalry week, the race for playoff positions is tightening, and the NFL is putting football on the table before the turkey even arrives. **Week 12 begins Wednesday night, November 25, and continues through Monday, November 30.** No NFL team is on bye this week—but manager availability, early locks and injury decisions make this scheduling challenge unusually unforgiving.

## Your Five Thanksgiving Rivalries

{{week12_matchup_cards}}

### New York Noodles vs. HOU You Like That — The Undefeated Dinner Guest

New York is 11–0, but Houston's 275.10-point performance and league-high Week 11 offense offer a warning. New York's defensive response may be the difference between a spotless record and an upset.

### Tampa Bay Tyranny vs. Utah Yetis — The Leftovers Rematch

Utah's upset over NJz may be the season's turning point for a team now 5–6. Tampa Bay has explosive offense but needs more from its IDP lineup to turn individual box-score highs into victories.

### Good Things We OKC! vs. Seattle Supersonics — The Thanksgiving Main Course

Third versus fourth, with an 8–3 franchise facing a 7–4 one. Both are trying to improve playoff position, and Seattle's defense and OKC's offense were responsible for their respective Week 11 successes.

### LALALALA vs. Dalla Dalla Dallas — The Must-Win Plate

LALALALA (5–6) needs to keep playoff hopes from souring; Dallas (2–9) can still exact rivalry revenge and disrupt the final seeding picture.

### NJz Bunniez vs. District of Daebak — The Redemption Dessert

NJz entered Week 11 as a contender and exited with the league-low score. District has been better than its record some weeks, but at 3–8 it needs wins rather than close losses.

## Three Storylines That Make This Week Different

**1. Time windows, not bye weeks, are the new opponent.** Week 11 depleted lineups with six NFL teams idle. Week 12 restores all 32 NFL teams, but demands earlier-than-usual lineup choices and contingency planning.

**2. The contenders are no longer comfortable.** NJz's Week 11 stumble puts OKC level at 8–3 and Seattle just one game behind. An upset at Thanksgiving can dramatically change the race for playoff byes.

**3. NFL injuries may reshape the early games.** Kenneth Walker III’s groin status remains under review ahead of Thursday night, Josh Jacobs enters Wednesday with a finger injury designation and Sam Darnold is uncertain for Sunday. The current [official NFL injury report](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-week-12-injury-report-2026-season) is more important than a frozen preview for any actual start/sit decision.

## The Holiday Rivalry Challenge

Make Week 12 feel like a five-course event. Wednesday's opener is the appetizer, Thursday's tripleheader serves the centerpiece, Friday's Broncos–Steelers game is the leftovers round, and the final Sunday–Monday slate settles the rivalry. Every franchise will have a chance to celebrate—or spend Thanksgiving explaining its lineup decisions.
