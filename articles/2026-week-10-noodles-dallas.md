# Ten and Counting: Dallas Lets the Undefeated Noodles Off the Hook
**New York Noodles 213.16 · Dalla Dalla Dallas 204.79 | Final**

New York entered Week 10 at **9–0** after demolishing OKC with **302.25** points a week earlier. Against two-win Dallas, it looked like the sort of matchup that might require little drama. Instead, the Noodles crashed to **213.16** — a drop of **89.09 points** — and finished only seventh in weekly scoring. Yet the unbeaten streak survived by **8.37**, giving New York its tenth consecutive victory and perhaps its clearest warning that a strong record is no substitute for a strong lineup.

**Devin Lloyd (24.60)** and **George Karlaftis (20.30)** kept the defensive side afloat, while **Josh Allen (22.34)** and **Bhayshul Tuten (18.80)** provided enough on offense. But there was little margin for error: **Drake Maye** managed **3.02** in the superflex spot and **Denzel Boston** contributed **2.10** at wide receiver. New York's defensive starters scored **109.10**, its offense **104.06** — neither unit resembling the one that had overwhelmed opponents throughout much of the season.

Dallas' near-miss was far more painful. **Deshaun Watson (21.38)**, **Jayden Daniels (20.76)**, **Andrew Van Ginkel (20.90)** and **Xavier McKinney (19.75)** all did their part. But **Dallas Goedert** and **Akeem Davis-Gaither** returned zeros in starting slots. **George Pickens exploded for 38.00 on the bench**; the export identifies a legal Pickens-for-Dylan Sampson switch worth **32.30**, more than enough to erase an 8.37-point deficit. **Mike Green (27.70)** and **Jaelan Phillips (27.65)** also sat unused. Dallas' calculated optimal lineup reached **319.44** against an actual **204.79**, the largest potential lineup gap in the league this week.

At **10–0**, New York still controls the race. At **2–8**, Dallas is left with a stark what-if: it came within one possession's worth of fantasy points of the season's defining upset, and an extraordinary amount of unused production stayed on the bench.
