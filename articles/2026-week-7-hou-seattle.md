# HOU Brings the Firepower

**Final: HOU You Like That 296.81, Seattle Supersonics 214.57. Margin: 82.24 points.** HOU had been looking for a result to change the conversation around its season. Week 7 provided one in convincing fashion: **the highest score in the league this week**, handed to a Seattle side that entered the matchup in the upper half of the standings.

## HOU You Like That: Quarterbacks Set the Tone

The foundation was a strong two-quarterback combination. **Joe Burrow scored 28.88**, while **Dak Prescott added 23.28**. **Tee Higgins (23.80)**, **Omar Cooper Jr. (20.00)**, **Boye Mafe (19.85)** and **Amon-Ra St. Brown (19.70)** kept the production flowing. This was not merely an offense running hot; the output included contributions throughout the IDP lineup.

HOU improved to **2–5**. That's still a difficult position in the table, but an almost 300-point week shows a ceiling that its record alone doesn't reveal. With **1,701.56 points for** through Week 7, the roster has performed better than a quick glance at the win column might suggest.

## Seattle Supersonics: Good Performances, Wrong Opponent

Seattle put up **214.57**, powered by **Luther Burden III (21.70)**, **Nakobe Dean (21.25)**, **Kirk Cousins (20.40)** and **Jared Goff (18.02)**. In isolation, these are useful scores. Against HOU's explosion, they weren't remotely enough.

There were also alternatives on the bench, including **Malik Willis (26.02)** and **Keisean Nixon (20.00)**. The optimal-lineup calculation estimated **53.90 points left on the bench**, but even recovering those points would not erase the entire 82.24-point deficit. Seattle slipped to **4–3**.

## What This Means

HOU now has a benchmark for what its roster can look like when the quarterback and skill-position production align. Seattle must treat this as a setback rather than proof of collapse: sometimes an opponent simply turns in the week's strongest performance. The next test is whether HOU can repeat this level and whether Seattle can return to form.
